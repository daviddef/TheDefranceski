## How to get them out
Downloads are blocked from this browser and the download endpoint needs the
session, so they cannot be pulled here one by one at any sensible cost.
The practical routes, in order:
1. **Family Tree Builder** (free desktop app) — sync the site and every photo
   lands in a local folder with its filename and caption intact.
2. **Photos → album → Download** in the web app, which zips a whole album.
3. Point me at a handful by photoID and I will pull those individually.
