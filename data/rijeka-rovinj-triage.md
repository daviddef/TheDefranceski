# Rijeka and Rovinj on FamilySearch: what is on the film, what the record index points at, what has been read

Written 23 November 2026 from local data only (Record Atlas `fs-volumes.json`, this archive's `searched.json` and `familysearch-croatia.csv`). No FamilySearch call was made for it. Not a coverage claim: a map for choosing pages.

**Scale.** Rijeka: 50 volumes, about 17,600 images. Rovinj: 29 volumes, about 12,100 images, and Rovinjsko Selo 3 more. Together about 29,700 images, i.e. roughly 930 reader runs of 32 images. They cannot be read end to end in one session, and nothing in this file is, or may be written as, "walked".

**What has been read** (all `walked:false`, always at named images, never a volume end to end): Rijeka births and marriages 9RKB-7M3 (1626 entry), 9R2X-ZNT, 9R2X-C62, 9R2X-82Q, 9R2X-DPX, 9R2X-DP6; Rijeka deaths 9R26-YWG, 9R26-YWL; Rovinj deaths 9R2X-VZ9 (images 190, 203, 216-228 of 645); Rovinj marriages 9R2X-168 (image 148). Every other Rijeka and Rovinj volume has no row at all.
