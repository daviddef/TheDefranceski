## Subject

Request for copies — Tracing and documentation case no. 349.822,
DEFRANCESKI, Peter, born 15.06.1913

## Body

Dear Arolsen Archives,

I am writing as a descendant of the Defranceski family of Crikvenica, Croatia, to
request copies of the documents in one of your case files.

**The file I am asking for**

- Reference code: **06030302.0.349.822**
- Title: Tracing and documentation case no. 349.822 for DEFRANCESKI, PETER born 15.06.1913
- Collection: 6.3.3.2 — Repository of T/D cases
- 8 documents, creation date 1954–1984

Your online archive shows the file exists and states that parts of it are not
displayed online for data protection reasons. I would be grateful for copies of
whatever you are able to release, and for a note of what is withheld and why.

**The person**

Petar (Peter) Defranceski, born 15 June 1913 at Crikvenica, then Austria-Hungary,
later Yugoslavia. Yugoslav national. Ship's engineer, later a motor mechanic
(Auto-Mechaniker). He left Yugoslavia for Germany in April 1939 and did not return.

I have already read the following in your online collections, so there is no need
to send them again:

- IRO identity no. **148.902**, category CB, later "Bj"
- CM/1 personal file, 3.2.1.1 (application of 7 November 1947, 6 documents)
- Displaced-persons folder **DP0758**, reference 03010101 05 070 — the French
  occupation cards for him, his wife and children
- The counselling card of 22.7.1949 / 28.4.1951 / 25.5.1951
- The IRO "Changements de statut" form Tit/CM 1165 of 11.8.1949

**What I would particularly like to know**

The T/D file spans thirty years, from 1954 to 1984, which suggests correspondence
rather than wartime documents. If the file shows it, I would be grateful to know:

1. **Who opened the case, and who else wrote to you about him** — a relative, a
   government office, a pension or compensation authority, or Petar himself.
2. **Any address for him after 1951.** The last address I have is
   Kelterstraße 30, Neuhausen, Kreis Reutlingen (as at 1 April 1951).
3. **Any date or place of death.**
4. **Anything on his family** — his wife Luise, born Salzer at Neuhausen on
   5 October 1919, recorded on your index cards as *apatride*; and their two
   daughters, born 29 October 1940 at Bad Urach and 25 July 1945 at Metzingen.
5. **Any reference to his brothers** — Zdravko Defranceski (b. 1921, IRO 148.901,
   emigrated to Australia 8 March 1949) or Ivan Defranceski (b. 1925, entered work
   at Reutlingen 21 October 1941 and returned to Yugoslavia).

**A related question**

Zdravko Defranceski's index card is stamped "CM/1 destroyed". If any record survives
elsewhere in your holdings of that family's IRO file, or of their emigration to
Australia in March 1949, I would be glad to hear of it.

I am happy to pay any reproduction or research fee. Please let me know the amount
and how to settle it.

With thanks for your work,

David Defranceski
Brisbane, Australia
david.defranceski@gmail.com
